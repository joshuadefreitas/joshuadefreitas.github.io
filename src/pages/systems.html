Independent explorations in autonomous agents, economics, scientific computing, world intelligence, creative media, games and security.

Active research systems

SekiyaActive

Autonomous Agents & Strategic Interaction

Software for studying how autonomous agents behave when they have to decide together: who cooperates, who free-rides, and what changes when others can see what you did. It runs controlled games between simulated agents.

Illustration: simulated agents in a public-goods game

LathildaActive

Quantitative Economics & Forecasting

Research on economics and forecasting, mostly about evaluation: what a forecaster could really have known at the time, and whether a model beats a simple baseline. Careful tests, including the ones that come out negative.

Illustration: forecast fans over a synthetic series

PalinoirActive

Scientific Computing & Simulation

Simulation and numerical computing, from reaction–diffusion patterns to lattice models, run on GPUs and checked so that the same code gives the same numbers on different machines.

Illustration: a reaction–diffusion simulation, computed live in your browser
